# S8 report — Visual polish          Date: Wed 11 Nov 2026

## Done
- **Consistency (§1):**
  - **One header pattern for full-screen flows:** a round back button in the top bar with an `.h1` underneath. Booking status, Payment, Manage spot and Finish setup used an `h2` inside the top bar; they now match the others. Tab screens are `.h1` plus a sub-line.
  - **Payment's "Pay" button moved to a bottom bar.** It was half off-screen at the end of the scrolling form.
  - Tabular numbers on every changing figure (prices, balances, ring %, timers, kWh, badges, chart labels).
  - Destructive buttons are now red-**tinted** (Reset confirm, Cancel/Stop confirm) instead of solid red.
- **Motion (§2):**
  - Route transitions in `lib/router.tsx`: forward = slide in from the right (240ms), back = from the left, tab/replace = fade (200ms). Screens enter from **50% opacity, never 0**, so there is no blank frame.
  - Skeleton placeholders (shimmer) for the lazy screens instead of a bare spinner.
  - The earnings balance counts up.
  - A star tap bounce, alongside the existing check pop, button press scale and ring easing.
  - Everything is covered by the global `prefers-reduced-motion` override.
- **Photos (§3):** `public/photos/manifest.json` (empty) and `public/photos/README.md` (naming, 1200×800 / <250 KB, manifest format, credit lines). `SpotPhoto` shows a manifest photo when one exists for that spot and slot, and the illustration otherwise; a missing or broken file falls back silently. The illustrations now have depth (light from the top, a darker floor edge and a vignette). A "Photo credits" section was added to the main README. **No images were downloaded or hot-linked.**
- **Installed-app feel (§4):**
  - `public/manifest.webmanifest`: standalone, black theme and background, `start_url`/`scope` under `/plug-and-park/`.
  - Icons at 192 and 512 (maskable-safe) plus a 180px `apple-touch-icon`, generated by `scripts/make-icons.mjs`: a white bolt on `--blue`.
  - The iOS meta tags.
  - No service worker.
- **Accessibility (§5):**
  - Sheets move focus inside, trap Tab, close on Escape and return focus to the opener.
  - **44px hit areas** for chips, the sheet handle and the Price/Rating toggle (invisible `::after` extension, same look); text links have a 44px minimum height.
  - Contrast checked: muted on surface 4.86:1, amber badge text 4.66:1, blue-deep on white, and black on blue.
- **Performance (§6):** Leaflet lives only in the lazy map/apply chunks (checked at runtime: the landing page requests no Leaflet code). Fonts use `display=swap`.

## Known bugs / risks
- `scrollIntoView` and focus can't move the frame any more (`overflow: clip`). Older iOS (< 16) ignores `clip` and treats it as visible, so check on a real device (below).

## Decisions made
- Route transitions start at 50% opacity instead of 0. This trades a slightly softer fade for a guarantee of no blank frame.
- Icons: a white bolt on `--blue` (the same mark as the favicon) rather than serif "P&P", so it stays readable at 48px.
